# Nexus AI: Screenshot & Demo Checklist

To fully leverage this project for a PM portfolio, you must capture visual evidence of the Trust Engineering features. Use this checklist to capture the exact screenshots and GIFs needed for your README, LinkedIn, and Portfolio site.

## 📸 Core Screenshots

- [ ] **1. The Vault (Dashboard)**
  * **What to capture:** The main dashboard showing Total Net Worth, Unrealized P&L, and the Plotly Performance Benchmark chart.
  * **Why it matters:** Proves the UI is clean, functional, and tracking real math.

- [ ] **2. Proactive Smart Alerts**
  * **What to capture:** A triggered Smart Alert showing "Portfolio Drift Detected" or an unread Daily AI Digest.
  * **Why it matters:** Demonstrates the shift from reactive chatbots to an anticipatory operating system.

- [ ] **3. Scenario Simulator Modal**
  * **What to capture:** The modal after selecting a scenario (e.g., "Market Crash"). Show both the mathematical impact (`-12% drop`) and the AI's explanation of *why*.
  * **Why it matters:** Shows the exact separation of deterministic math vs. probabilistic reasoning.

- [ ] **4. AI Confidence Trace**
  * **What to capture:** The "Trust Interface" / Confidence Score pill expanded to show the signal data and timestamp used by the LLM.
  * **Why it matters:** The ultimate proof of "Trust Engineering" and explainability.

## 🎥 Demo Video / GIF Flow (Recommended: 30-45 seconds)

1. **Start** on the Vault Dashboard, showing the Nifty 50 benchmark chart.
2. **Click** into "AI Strategies" to trigger the Daily Digest. 
3. **Hover** over an AI Confidence score to show the tooltip/trace.
4. **Open** the Scenario Simulator, select "Tech Rally," and hit simulate to show the instantaneous deterministic math + AI response.

## 💡 Pro-Tips for Capture
* Ensure Docker is running smoothly (`docker-compose up -d`) before recording.
* Use a tool like **CleanShot X** or **Loom** for crisp, high-framerate GIFs.
* Resize your browser to a standard 16:9 ratio (e.g., 1440x900) so the screenshots fit perfectly into LinkedIn carousels.
